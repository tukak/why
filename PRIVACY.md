# Privacy policy

Why? does not collect, send or share any data.

- The app has no internet permission. It cannot send data anywhere.
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- It notices only when the phone unlocks and when the screen turns off. It does not read which apps you use.
- Your answers, reasons and settings stay in the app's private storage on your phone.
- If Android backup is on, Android can include this data in your own device backup or transfer it to a new phone. The developer has no access to it.
- Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything.
